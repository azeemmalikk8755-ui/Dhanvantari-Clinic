Dr. Debajyoti Banerjee

Consultant Pediatrician & Neonatologist

  • Education: MBBS (Gold Medalist), MD Pediatrics (Himalayan Hospital, UK)
  • Experience: Ex. Senior Resident – Himalayan Hospital, UK | Ex. AIIMS Rishikesh | Ex. ITBP Force
  • Registration: UKMC 13312
  • Memberships: National Neonatology Forum, Indian Academy of Pediatrics

Our Clinic's Story

Dhanvantari Clinic is proudly established as Vistapith, Athoorwala's first Specialised Neonatal & Pediatric Clinic. We brought specialized child care near your doorstep because we believe every child in the hills of Uttarakhand deserves immediate access to expert medical attention.

Our mission is centered around nurturing healthier newborns, infants, and children. Whether it is routine check-ups, precise diagnostics, or advanced emergency readiness, our facility is equipped to support your family's healthcare journey in a warm and friendly environment.
