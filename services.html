Our Services

Comprehensive, expert care tailored for every stage of your child's growth

Consultation & Diagnostics

1. OPD Consult

Expert consultation for newborns, children & adolescents. We provide a thorough evaluation of symptoms, patient history, and create personalized management plans for common and complex childhood illnesses.

2. Diagnostics

Accurate & reliable tests for timely diagnosis. We utilize precise testing protocols to identify underlying issues quickly, ensuring that the correct treatment begins as soon as possible without unnecessary delays.

Newborn & Child Care

3. Neonatal Care

Specialized care for newborns, including premature & sick babies. Guided by a seasoned neonatologist, we ensure delicate immune systems and fragile newborn bodies receive the safest, most attentive care required for early stability.

4. Pediatric Care

Comprehensive medical care for infants, children & growing kids. From seasonal infections and digestive issues to allergies and general wellness, we offer dedicated treatment modalities for children up to 18 years of age.

8. Nebulisation

Effective relief from cough, wheeze & breathing difficulties. For pediatric asthma and severe respiratory tract infections, we offer immediate nebulisation support to help your child breathe easy and recover faster.

Preventive, Growth & Nutrition

5. Vaccination

Safe & effective vaccinations as per latest IAP guidelines. Immunization is critical to disease prevention. We provide a complete schedule of vaccines in a child-friendly, hygienic environment to minimize pain and anxiety.

6. Well Child Check Ups

Regular check-ups for healthy growth & early detection of issues. Preventive visits allow us to track physical milestones, offer parental counseling, and catch potential health concerns before they become problematic.

10. Growth Monitoring

Regular growth tracking to ensure your child is growing right. We measure height, weight, and head circumference at each visit, comparing them against standard pediatric percentiles to ensure nutritional adequacy.

12. Structured Dietary Plans

Personalized & balanced diet plans for healthy growth & nutrition. Based on your child’s specific age, weight, and metabolic needs, we design practical, culturally appropriate meal strategies that promote optimal brain and body development.

Developmental & Emergency Services

7. Developmental & Behavioural Monitoring

Tracking milestones & behaviour for a better tomorrow. We actively monitor cognitive, emotional, and social development, offering early intervention strategies for behavioral concerns or developmental delays.

9. Adolescent Health

Care & guidance for physical, mental & emotional well-being. The teenage years bring unique physiological and psychological challenges; we provide a safe, confidential space for adolescents to address puberty, stress, and lifestyle habits.

11. Advanced Neonatal & Pediatric Resuscitation

Advanced life support training & emergency care readiness. Our clinic is equipped and staff is highly trained in advanced resuscitation protocols to handle critical pediatric emergencies with immediate, life-saving precision.
