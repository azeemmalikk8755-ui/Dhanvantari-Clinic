A Complete Newborn Care & Vaccination Guide for New Parents in Dehradun

Bringing a new baby home is one of the most joyous moments in a parent’s life, but it can also be overwhelming. If you are looking for a reliable pediatrician in Dehradun, or simply want to understand the basics of infant care, this guide is for you. Below, we discuss essential newborn care tips, the importance of the vaccination schedule, and when it is time to consult a child specialist in Dehradun.

The First Weeks After Birth: Essential Newborn Care Tips

The neonatal period (the first 28 days of life) is critical. As the best neonatologist Dehradun has to offer in the Athoorwala region, Dhanvantari Clinic recommends focusing on a few fundamental areas:

  • Feeding: Breast milk is the best source of nutrition for your newborn. It contains vital antibodies that protect against infections. Ensure your baby is feeding 8 to 12 times a day.
  • Sleep Safety: Newborns sleep a lot—often 16 to 17 hours a day. Always place your baby on their back to sleep on a firm mattress to reduce the risk of SIDS (Sudden Infant Death Syndrome).
  • Hygiene and Cord Care: Keep the umbilical cord stump clean and dry. Give sponge baths until the stump falls off, and always wash your hands before handling your newborn to prevent infections.

Understanding the Vaccination Schedule for Infants in India

Immunization is your child's shield against severe, preventable diseases. At our pediatric clinic in Athoorwala, we strictly follow the latest Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) vaccination guidelines.

Birth to 6 Weeks: Your baby should receive the BCG (Tuberculosis), OPV (Oral Polio Vaccine), and Hepatitis B vaccines at birth. At 6 weeks, combinations covering DTP (Diphtheria, Tetanus, Pertussis), Rotavirus, and Pneumococcal vaccines begin.

Why is it important? Delaying vaccines leaves your child vulnerable. Regular well-child check-ups ensure that your child is immunized on time and their growth metrics (height, weight, head circumference) are tracking properly.

Warning Signs: When to Visit a Pediatrician

While many infant behaviors are normal, certain signs warrant an immediate visit to a pediatric specialist. Watch out for:

  • Fever above 100.4°F (38°C) in babies under 3 months
  • Poor feeding or sudden loss of appetite
  • Signs of dehydration (fewer wet diapers, dry mouth)
  • Difficulty breathing or persistent coughing/wheezing

If you notice any of these signs, prompt nebulisation or advanced neonatal care may be required.
